    60 seconds. The finished structured records support a network. Each line links a House filer to a stock reported as purchased. The shared-stock analysis retains tickers with at least two filers and counts repeated purchases once per filer-ticker connection. AMZN connects 19 filers, the highest stock degree in this extract. The interactive project also has committee views; those committee links are outside the simple shared-stock metrics quoted here. This is a separate frozen House-only purchase extract, not the P3 House-Senate joined table. Incomplete extraction and excluded transaction types limit the findings, and overlap alone is not evidence of coordination.
